## Brand & Style
The design system reflects the convergence of institutional journalistic rigor and contemporary high-yield enterprise across Central-West Brazil. The interface communicates speed, authority, and financial precision without sacrificing regional relevance. The aesthetic blends Corporate Modern with High-Contrast Editorial: authoritative deep navy foundations, crisp hairline delineations, broadcast-grade metric badges, and targeted accents of vibrant agricultural green. 

Every view evokes the gravitas of a major terminal and the clarity of a modern news desk. Visual clutter is eliminated in favor of clean typographic hierarchy, structured data grids, real-time market telemetry, and high-legibility reportage layout.

## Colors
The color palette anchors editorial credibility through high contrast and deliberate functional zoning:

- **Primary (`#0E1B2B` - Deep Slate Navy):** The structural core. Drives the dark navigation headers, high-impact hero article containers, typographic titles, and primary editorial framing. Deepened to `#0B1522` for high-density command bars and immersive dark containers.
- **Secondary (`#23B26D` - Vibrant Agro Green):** The kinetic economic signal. Applied intentionally to agribusiness telemetry, positive financial delta indicators, live broadcast badges, active market states, and high-priority breaking news indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#3B729E` - Steel Blue):** Mid-tone anchor used for metadata, datelines, secondary navigation items, contextual category tags, and informational data charts. Complemented by Light Sky (`#78A6CD`) for interactive hover states and dark-mode accents.
- **Neutral Palette:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the dominant canvas layer for editorial reading. Slate neutral (`#64748B`) drives secondary copy and metadata. Soft Sand / Light Gray (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) defines section backdrops, card surfaces, and alternating data tables, paired with subtle borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances the muscular, contemporary presence of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and market headings with the clean utilitarian scanning capacity of Inter for long-form regional journalism. 

- **Headlines & Display:** Set tightly with negative tracking to convey broadcast impact and journalistic density. Headlines on dark hero blocks employ `#FFFFFF`, while light canvas headlines strictly utilize `#0E1B2B`.
- **Editorial Body:** Scaled at 18px (`body-lg`) with 30px line height on long-form reports to eliminate fatigue during reading.
- **Overlines & Micro-Badges:** Transformed into uppercase (`label-sm` and `label-md`) with deliberate positive letter tracking to create immediate visual differentiation for breaking tags, region labels, and ticker metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured for multi-tier news delivery:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 2rem outer canvas margin, capping at a maximum width of 1440px. Supports asynchronous multi-zone layouts: a 7-column primary news well, a 5-column secondary analysis stack, and dedicated 3-column agribusiness market rail widgets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, and 1.5rem margin. Side rails collapse below the hero block; tickers transition into touch-swipable rails.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1rem margin. Content reflows into a single linear hierarchy prioritizing breaking alerts, market ribbons, primary stories, and category feeds.

The vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px baseline grid system, enforcing consistent spacing across card padding, article section dividers, and real-time market strips.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through structured tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter hairlines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Base`: `#FFFFFF` on light canvas; `#0B1522` on dark editorial/hero surfaces.
  - `Elevated Surface 1`: `#F8FAFC` for secondary editorial wells and news feed modules.
  - `Elevated Surface 2`: `#F1F5F9` for market quote cards, tabular strips, and sidebar modules.
  - `High-Contrast Hero Layer`: A deep slate wash (`#0E1B2B`) framing lead investigative stories and state-level broadcast coverage.
- **Outlines & Borders:** Structural division relies on crisp 1px borders using `#E2E8F0` on light containers and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on deep navy blocks.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Reserved exclusively for floating elements (sticky navigation bars, real-time commodity tickers, and mobile contextual drawers). Shadows use a tinted navy footprint: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(14, 27, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(14, 27, 43, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The design system adopts a controlled `Soft` geometry (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) to project precision, speed, and institutional credibility. 
- **Editorial Media & Cards:** Set to `rounded-sm` (4px) or `rounded-md` (6px) to maintain a sharp, disciplined grid that resists the casual feel of heavily rounded apps.
- **Badges, Tickers, & Micro-Pills:** Utilize a subtle 2px to 4px radius, preserving clean architectural edges.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Standardized at 4px corners to communicate reliability, strength, and decisiveness.

## Components

- **Real-Time Agribusiness & Financial Ticker:**
  Full-bleed dark ribbon (`#0B1522`) anchored below the primary masthead. Displays commodities (Soybean, Corn, Cattle / Boi Gordo) and economic indexes. Indicators use `#23B26D` for gains and `#EF4444` for losses, accompanied by micro directional carets and `ticker-data` typography.
- **Live Broadcast & Breaking Badges:**
  High-visibility status tags. Breaking news uses solid `#0E1B2B` with a `#23B26D` left hairline accent or a subtle pulsing agro-green status dot. Text is styled in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Editorial & Business Cards:**
  - *Standard Feed Card:* Clean white surface with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 4px corner radius, category tag in `#3B729E`, title in `headline-sm`, and publication timestamp in `#64748B`. Hover state applies a 1px border shift to `#3B729E` and subtle title color transition.
  - *Hero Deep Slate Card:* Full-bleed navy block (`#0E1B2B`) with crisp white editorial typography, high-resolution imagery, and vibrant agro-green accents on tags and reading metrics.
- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid Deep Slate Navy (`#0E1B2B`) with white text, 4px border radius, 0.75rem vertical and 1.25rem horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#1E3147`.
  - *Accent / Action:* Vibrant Agro Green (`#23B26D`) fill with crisp white text for subscription actions, alert triggers, or live stream entry points.
  - *Secondary / Outline:* Crisp white background with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `#0E1B2B` text; transforms to `#F1F5F9` background on hover.
- **Chips & Category Navigation:**
  Compact horizontal filters for state regions (Goiânia, Anápolis, Rio Verde, etc.) and verticals (Agro, Economia, Política). Neutral state features `#F1F5F9` background with `#0E1B2B` text; active state inverts to `#0E1B2B` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Input Fields & Search Terminals:**
  Clean, high-density text fields with a 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` interior, 4px radius, and placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Focused states shift to a 1.5px `#3B729E` outline with zero exterior glow.
- **Lists & Data Tables:**
  Alternating soft sand (`#F8FAFC`) rows bordered by hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`), engineered for rapid reading of agricultural commodity quotes, local municipal tenders, and stock changes.
